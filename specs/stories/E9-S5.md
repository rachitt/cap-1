## Description

Good-to-have items 11 through 15 from the brief. The architecture document carries a rendered diagram, the TDD document records the test-first discipline actually followed, knowledge deposits capture reusable learning, and at least one fix loop is recorded with a full detect to validate trace. These are written late because the fix loop requires a real failure to have occurred.

## Acceptance Criteria

## Verification Steps

**`F263` — The architecture document contains a diagram covering all six layers** _(AC1, integration)_

1. Assert docs/architecture.md exists
1. Assert it contains a diagram block such as a mermaid graph or an embedded image reference
1. Assert the diagram names all six layers Types, Config, Repository, Service, API and UI
1. Assert the audit and notification modules are shown as cross-cutting

**`F264` — The TDD document includes a concrete red-then-green worked example** _(AC2, integration)_

1. Assert docs/tdd.md exists
1. Assert it contains a worked example naming a specific test and the implementation that followed
1. Assert it shows the failing state preceding the passing state
1. Assert it references the 80% coverage floor and the 100% target

**`F265` — A recorded fix loop documents the full detect-to-PR trace** _(AC3, integration)_

1. Assert at least one file exists under docs/fix-loops/
1. Assert it documents distinct detect, reproduce, fix and validate phases
1. Assert it references a real failing test or evaluator finding by identifier
1. Assert it links to the branch or merge that resolved it

**`F266` — Knowledge deposits are recorded and cross-referenced to learned rules** _(AC4, integration)_

1. Assert docs/knowledge-deposits.md exists and contains at least one dated entry
1. Assert each entry states a reusable lesson rather than a task log line
1. Assert it cross-references .claude/state/learned-rules.md

**`F267` — At least two sprint cycles are evidenced by contracts and evaluator outputs** _(AC4, integration)_

1. List files under sprint-contracts/ and assert at least 2 sprint contracts exist
1. List files under specs/reviews/ and assert at least 2 evaluator outputs exist
1. Assert the outputs are from distinct iterations showing a ratcheting improvement in score or pass count
