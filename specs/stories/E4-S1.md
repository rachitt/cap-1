## Description

The third of four mandated domain policy files, pure and dependency-free. It encodes only rules the brief states: a strictly positive amount of at most two decimal places (DR-14), no self-transfer (DR-15), a destination that exists and is active (DR-16), and sufficient funds or else InsufficientFundsException (DR-18, AC-05). It imposes no beneficiary precondition (DR-17) and no cap or velocity limit (DR-19).

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC1.** The policy rejects a zero, negative or greater-than-2dp amount, raising a domain error for `Decimal('0.00')`, `Decimal('-5.00')` and `Decimal('10.001')` (DR-14, edge cases E-05, E-06).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F086` | functional | Zero and negative transfer amounts are rejected by the policy |
| `F087` | functional | An amount with more than two decimal places is rejected rather than silently rounded |

**AC2.** A transfer whose source and destination account are the same raises a self-transfer error (DR-15, edge case E-02), and a destination account with `status = CLOSED` or one that does not exist is rejected (DR-16, edge case E-03).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F088` | functional | A self-transfer is rejected with an error code distinct from insufficient funds |
| `F089` | functional | A closed or non-existent destination account is rejected |

**AC3.** An amount exceeding the available balance raises `InsufficientFundsException` with `error_code = 'INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS'`; a transfer of exactly the full balance is permitted, since DR-11 imposes no minimum balance (AC-05, DR-18, edge case E-01).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F090` | functional | An amount above the balance raises InsufficientFundsException |
| `F091` | functional | A transfer of exactly the full available balance is permitted with no minimum-balance floor |

**AC4.** The policy imposes no beneficiary precondition (DR-17): an otherwise valid transfer to an active account that is not a saved beneficiary of the sender is allowed, and the module references no beneficiary concept.

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F092` | functional | A transfer to a valid active non-beneficiary account is permitted by the policy |
| `F093` | integration | The transfer policy module contains no beneficiary check |

**AC5.** The policy imposes no transfer cap, daily limit or velocity control (DR-19): a single large transfer within the available balance is permitted, and the module contains no cap parameter or threshold literal.

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F094` | functional | A large transfer within balance is permitted with no cap applied |
| `F095` | integration | No cap, limit or velocity threshold appears in the transfer policy module |

## Verification Steps

**`F086` — Zero and negative transfer amounts are rejected by the policy** _(AC1, functional)_

1. Evaluate the transfer policy with amount=Decimal('0.00') and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Evaluate with amount=Decimal('-5.00') and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Assert neither call returns an allow decision

**`F087` — An amount with more than two decimal places is rejected rather than silently rounded** _(AC1, functional)_

1. Evaluate the transfer policy with amount=Decimal('10.001')
1. Assert a MoneyPrecisionError or DomainError subclass is raised
1. Assert the policy does not return an allow decision carrying a rounded 10.00 amount

**`F088` — A self-transfer is rejected with an error code distinct from insufficient funds** _(AC2, functional)_

1. Evaluate the policy with source_account_id equal to destination_account_id
1. Assert a DomainError subclass identifying a self-transfer is raised
1. Assert the error_code is distinct from 'INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS'

**`F089` — A closed or non-existent destination account is rejected** _(AC2, functional)_

1. Evaluate the policy with a destination account whose status is AccountStatus.CLOSED and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Evaluate the policy with a destination account that does not exist and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Evaluate the same case with an existing ACTIVE destination and assert no error is raised

**`F090` — An amount above the balance raises InsufficientFundsException** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Evaluate the policy with balance=Decimal('100.00') and amount=Decimal('100.01')
1. Assert InsufficientFundsException is raised
1. Assert the exception error_code equals 'INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS'

**`F091` — A transfer of exactly the full available balance is permitted with no minimum-balance floor** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Evaluate the policy with balance=Decimal('100.00') and amount=Decimal('100.00')
1. Assert no exception is raised and the policy returns an allow decision
1. Assert no minimum-balance rule blocks draining the account to 0.00

**`F092` — A transfer to a valid active non-beneficiary account is permitted by the policy** _(AC4, functional)_

1. Evaluate the policy with a valid ACTIVE destination account that the sender has not saved as a beneficiary
1. Assert no exception is raised
1. Assert the policy returns an allow decision

**`F093` — The transfer policy module contains no beneficiary check** _(AC4, integration)_

1. Run: grep -niE 'beneficiar' backend/src/domain/transfer_policy.py
1. Assert the command exits with status 1 (no matches)
1. Inspect the policy function signature and assert it accepts no beneficiary parameter

**`F094` — A large transfer within balance is permitted with no cap applied** _(AC5, functional)_

1. Evaluate the policy with balance=Decimal('1000000.00') and amount=Decimal('999999.99')
1. Assert no exception is raised and the policy returns an allow decision
1. Assert no cap-exceeded error type exists in the raised-error surface

**`F095` — No cap, limit or velocity threshold appears in the transfer policy module** _(AC5, integration)_

1. Run: grep -niE 'cap|daily|velocity|limit' backend/src/domain/transfer_policy.py
1. Assert the command exits with status 1 (no matches)
1. Inspect the policy function signature and assert it accepts no daily_cap or limit parameter
