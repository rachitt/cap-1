## Description

The core of AC-04 and AC-05 and the highest-severity code in the system. One SQLite transaction encloses the locked balance re-read, policy evaluation, debit, credit, transaction rows, audit entry and notification enqueue (DR-20). Any policy failure rolls the whole thing back leaving zero money moved. Note this service does not depend on beneficiaries: a transfer targets any valid active account (DR-17).

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC1.** A successful transfer of `Decimal('250.00')` from a 1000.00 account leaves the source at `750.00` and the destination at its prior balance plus `250.00`, with total money across both accounts unchanged (AC-04).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F108` | functional | A successful transfer debits the source and credits the destination by the exact amount |
| `F109` | functional | A successful transfer writes exactly one DEBIT and one CREDIT transaction sharing a transfer_id |

**AC2.** A transfer to a valid active account that is not a saved beneficiary of the sender succeeds, confirming beneficiaries do not gate transfers (DR-17, AC-04).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F110` | functional | A transfer to a non-beneficiary active account completes successfully |

**AC3.** A transfer exceeding the balance raises `InsufficientFundsException` and rolls everything back: both balances are unchanged, no transaction rows are written, and no notification is enqueued (AC-05, DR-18, edge case E-01).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F111` | functional | An insufficient-funds transfer raises InsufficientFundsException and moves zero money |
| `F112` | integration | A failed transfer writes no transaction rows and enqueues no notification |
| `F113` | functional | A failed transfer is still recorded as a FAILED transfer row with a failure_code and audited |

**AC4.** A successful transfer appends exactly one audit entry with `action='TRANSFER_COMPLETED'` and enqueues exactly one notification, both committed in the same transaction as the balance changes (AC-09, AC-10, DR-20).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F114` | integration | A committed transfer produces exactly one audit entry and one queued notification atomically |

**AC5.** Two concurrent transfers from the same account, each individually affordable but jointly exceeding the balance, result in exactly one success and one `InsufficientFundsException`, and the final balance is never negative (DR-20, edge case E-07).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F115` | integration | Concurrent double-spend attempts serialise so exactly one succeeds and the balance never goes negative |

## Verification Steps

**`F108` — A successful transfer debits the source and credits the destination by the exact amount** _(AC1, functional)_

1. Fund account A with 1000.00 and account B with 0.00
1. Execute a transfer of Decimal('250.00') from A to B
1. Assert A's balance is exactly Decimal('750.00') and B's balance is exactly Decimal('250.00')
1. Assert the sum of both balances equals the pre-transfer sum of 1000.00

**`F109` — A successful transfer writes exactly one DEBIT and one CREDIT transaction sharing a transfer_id** _(AC1, functional)_

1. Execute a successful transfer and capture the returned transfer id
1. Query transactions filtered by that transfer_id
1. Assert exactly 2 rows exist, one DEBIT on the source and one CREDIT on the destination
1. Assert both rows carry the same amount and correct balance_after values

**`F110` — A transfer to a non-beneficiary active account completes successfully** _(AC2, functional)_

1. Fund account A and create account B owned by a different customer, with no beneficiary saved by A
1. Execute a transfer of Decimal('50.00') from A to B
1. Assert the transfer status is COMPLETED and no error is raised
1. Assert B's balance increased by exactly 50.00

**`F111` — An insufficient-funds transfer raises InsufficientFundsException and moves zero money** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Fund account A with 100.00
1. Attempt a transfer of Decimal('500.00') inside pytest.raises(InsufficientFundsException)
1. Assert the exception is raised
1. Re-read both accounts in a new session and assert A is still 100.00 and the destination is unchanged

**`F112` — A failed transfer writes no transaction rows and enqueues no notification** _(AC3, integration)_

1. Attempt a transfer that fails on insufficient funds
1. Query the transactions table for rows referencing either account after the attempt
1. Assert no new DEBIT or CREDIT row was created
1. Assert the notifications table has no new row for that customer

**`F113` — A failed transfer is still recorded as a FAILED transfer row with a failure_code and audited** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Attempt a transfer that fails on insufficient funds
1. Query the transfers table for the attempt
1. Assert one row exists with status == TransferStatus.FAILED and failure_code == 'INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS'
1. Assert an audit entry exists recording the failed attempt per DR-22

**`F114` — A committed transfer produces exactly one audit entry and one queued notification atomically** _(AC4, integration)_

1. Execute one successful transfer
1. Query audit_entries for entity_type='Transfer' and assert exactly one row with action == 'TRANSFER_COMPLETED'
1. Query notifications for the sender and assert exactly one new row with event_type == 'TRANSFER_COMPLETED'
1. Assert both rows carry the same correlation_id as the transfer request

**`F115` — Concurrent double-spend attempts serialise so exactly one succeeds and the balance never goes negative** _(AC5, integration)_

1. Fund account A with exactly 100.00
1. Launch two concurrent transfers of 80.00 each from A using threads
1. Assert exactly one call returns successfully and exactly one raises InsufficientFundsException
1. Re-read A's balance and assert it equals Decimal('20.00') and is not negative

---

_Epic context: Sprint group G3 (payments). Delivers AC-04, AC-05, AC-06 and the AC-10 notification module. Per DR-17 beneficiaries are an independent convenience feature and are NOT a precondition of a transfer; AC-04 permits a transfer to any valid active account. Per DR-19 no transfer cap, limit or velocity control exists. Atomicity is the single most important correctness property in the system._
