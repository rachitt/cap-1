## Description

The fourth mandated domain policy file, pure and dependency-free. It owns the legal state-transition matrix from DR-23, the mandatory non-empty decision reason from DR-25, and structural validation of application input per DR-24 (principal strictly positive and at most two decimal places, tenure a positive whole number of months). It deliberately encodes no eligibility threshold.

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC1.** The transition matrix permits exactly `APPLIED → UNDER_REVIEW`, `UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED`, `UNDER_REVIEW → REJECTED` and `APPROVED → DISBURSED`; every other ordered pair raises `IllegalLoanTransitionError` (DR-23, AC-07).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F125` | functional | The four legal loan transitions are permitted by the state matrix |
| `F126` | functional | Every transition outside the legal matrix raises IllegalLoanTransitionError |

**AC2.** `assert_decision_reason(reason)` raises for an empty string and for a whitespace-only string, and accepts a non-empty reason, so an administrator cannot approve or reject silently (DR-25, AC-08, edge case E-13).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F127` | functional | Empty and whitespace-only decision reasons are rejected |

**AC3.** Application input is validated for structural validity only: a principal of `Decimal('0.00')`, a negative principal, a principal with three decimal places, a zero tenure and a fractional tenure are each rejected (DR-24).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F128` | functional | Non-positive and over-precision loan principals are rejected as structurally invalid |
| `F129` | functional | Zero and fractional loan tenures are rejected as structurally invalid |

**AC4.** The policy imposes no eligibility threshold (DR-24): a very large principal with a long tenure and no declared income passes structural validation, and the module contains no income, ratio, minimum or maximum threshold.

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F130` | functional | A large principal with no declared income passes structural validation |
| `F131` | integration | No eligibility threshold or EMI ratio appears in the loan policy module |

**AC5.** The module is pure: it imports only `decimal`, `typing` and `backend/src/types`, performs no I/O, and `uv run mypy --strict src/domain/loan_eligibility_policy.py` reports zero errors.

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F132` | integration | The loan policy module is dependency-free and typechecks strictly |

## Verification Steps

**`F125` — The four legal loan transitions are permitted by the state matrix** _(AC1, functional)_

1. Assert assert_transition(APPLIED, UNDER_REVIEW) does not raise
1. Assert assert_transition(UNDER_REVIEW, APPROVED) does not raise
1. Assert assert_transition(UNDER_REVIEW, REJECTED) does not raise
1. Assert assert_transition(APPROVED, DISBURSED) does not raise

**`F126` — Every transition outside the legal matrix raises IllegalLoanTransitionError** _(AC1, functional)_

1. Assert assert_transition(APPLIED, DISBURSED) raises IllegalLoanTransitionError per edge case E-11
1. Assert assert_transition(REJECTED, APPROVED) raises IllegalLoanTransitionError per edge case E-12
1. Assert assert_transition(DISBURSED, UNDER_REVIEW) raises IllegalLoanTransitionError
1. Enumerate all ordered pairs of LoanStatus and assert exactly 4 do not raise

**`F127` — Empty and whitespace-only decision reasons are rejected** _(AC2, functional)_

1. Call assert_decision_reason('') and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Call assert_decision_reason('   ') and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Call assert_decision_reason('Income insufficient for requested tenure') and assert no error is raised

**`F128` — Non-positive and over-precision loan principals are rejected as structurally invalid** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Call the application validator with principal=Decimal('0.00') and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Call with principal=Decimal('-100.00') and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Call with principal=Decimal('1000.001') and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Call with principal=Decimal('1000.00') and a valid tenure and assert no error is raised

**`F129` — Zero and fractional loan tenures are rejected as structurally invalid** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Call the application validator with tenure_months=0 and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Call with tenure_months=-6 and assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Call with tenure_months=12 and assert no error is raised

**`F130` — A large principal with no declared income passes structural validation** _(AC4, functional)_

1. Call the application validator with principal=Decimal('99999999.00'), tenure_months=360 and declared_monthly_income=None
1. Assert no exception is raised
1. Assert the validator returns a valid result rather than an ineligible decision

**`F131` — No eligibility threshold or EMI ratio appears in the loan policy module** _(AC4, integration)_

1. Run: grep -niE 'income|ratio|emi|min_principal|max_principal|min_tenure|max_tenure|eligib' backend/src/domain/loan_eligibility_policy.py
1. Assert the command exits with status 1 (no matches)
1. Inspect the validator signature and assert it accepts no threshold or bound parameters

**`F132` — The loan policy module is dependency-free and typechecks strictly** _(AC5, integration)_

1. Run: grep -nE '^(from|import) ' backend/src/domain/loan_eligibility_policy.py
1. Assert every import references only decimal, typing, dataclasses, enum or src.types
1. Run: cd backend && uv run mypy --strict src/domain/loan_eligibility_policy.py and assert exit code 0
