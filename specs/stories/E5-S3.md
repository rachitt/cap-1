## Description

Orchestrates AC-07 and AC-08. Each decision validates the transition against the policy, requires a non-empty reason on approve and reject, appends a transition row and an audit entry, and enqueues a notification — because DR-30 requires a notification on every loan state transition without exception. Disbursement credits an existing active savings account of the applicant per DR-27.

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC1.** `apply_for_loan` creates an APPLIED loan, appends an audit entry with `action='LOAN_APPLIED'`, and enqueues a notification, all in one transaction (AC-07, AC-09, AC-10).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F139` | functional | Applying for a loan creates an APPLIED record with an audit entry and a notification |

**AC2.** Every loan state transition enqueues exactly one notification, so a loan taken APPLIED → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED → DISBURSED produces four notifications in total including the application (DR-30, AC-10).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F140` | integration | Each loan state transition produces exactly one notification with no gaps |
| `F141` | integration | Each loan state transition appends a matching audit entry |

**AC3.** `approve` and `reject` raise when the reason is empty or whitespace-only, and no transition row, audit entry or notification is written on that failed attempt (DR-25, AC-08, edge case E-13).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F142` | functional | Approving with an empty reason raises and writes no transition |
| `F143` | functional | Rejecting with a whitespace-only reason raises and writes no audit entry or notification |

**AC4.** An illegal transition such as approving an APPLIED loan or approving an already-REJECTED loan raises `IllegalLoanTransitionError` and leaves the loan status unchanged (edge cases E-11, E-12).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F144` | functional | Approving a loan that is not UNDER_REVIEW raises IllegalLoanTransitionError |
| `F145` | functional | Approving an already-rejected loan raises and leaves it REJECTED |

**AC5.** `disburse` credits an existing ACTIVE savings account belonging to the applicant by exactly the loan principal in the same transaction as the status change, and raises when the target account is closed (DR-27, edge case E-22).

| Feature | Category | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| `F146` | functional | Disbursement credits the applicant's active savings account by the loan principal |
| `F147` | functional | Disbursement to a closed account raises and moves no money |
| `F148` | security | Disbursement rejects an account that does not belong to the applicant |

## Verification Steps

**`F139` — Applying for a loan creates an APPLIED record with an audit entry and a notification** _(AC1, functional)_

1. Call apply_for_loan as customer A with a structurally valid principal and tenure
1. Assert the returned loan status is LoanStatus.APPLIED
1. Query audit_entries for entity_type='LoanApplication' and assert one entry with action == 'LOAN_APPLIED'
1. Query notifications for A and assert one new row exists for the application event

**`F140` — Each loan state transition produces exactly one notification with no gaps** _(AC2, integration)_

1. Apply for a loan, then move it to UNDER_REVIEW, APPROVED and DISBURSED in sequence
1. Query the notifications table for that customer filtered to loan events
1. Assert exactly 4 notification rows exist
1. Assert their event_type values cover the application and each of the three transitions

**`F141` — Each loan state transition appends a matching audit entry** _(AC2, integration)_

1. Take a loan through APPLIED, UNDER_REVIEW, APPROVED and DISBURSED
1. Query audit_entries for entity_type='LoanApplication' and that loan id
1. Assert exactly 4 entries exist, one per event
1. Assert each entry records the acting actor_id and the correlation_id

**`F142` — Approving with an empty reason raises and writes no transition** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Move a loan to UNDER_REVIEW, then call approve with reason='' inside pytest.raises
1. Assert a DomainError subclass is raised
1. Assert the loan status is still UNDER_REVIEW
1. Assert no new loan_state_transitions row was appended

**`F143` — Rejecting with a whitespace-only reason raises and writes no audit entry or notification** _(AC3, functional)_

1. Move a loan to UNDER_REVIEW, then call reject with reason='   ' inside pytest.raises
1. Assert the error is raised and the loan status is unchanged
1. Assert the audit entry count for that loan is unchanged
1. Assert the notification count for that customer is unchanged

**`F144` — Approving a loan that is not UNDER_REVIEW raises IllegalLoanTransitionError** _(AC4, functional)_

1. Create a loan in APPLIED status and call approve with a valid reason inside pytest.raises(IllegalLoanTransitionError)
1. Assert the error is raised
1. Re-read the loan and assert status is still APPLIED

**`F145` — Approving an already-rejected loan raises and leaves it REJECTED** _(AC4, functional)_

1. Take a loan to REJECTED with a valid reason
1. Call approve on it inside pytest.raises(IllegalLoanTransitionError)
1. Assert the error is raised and the loan status is still REJECTED

**`F146` — Disbursement credits the applicant's active savings account by the loan principal** _(AC5, functional)_

1. Take a loan of principal 5000.00 to APPROVED for a customer whose savings account holds 100.00
1. Call disburse targeting that account
1. Assert the loan status becomes DISBURSED
1. Assert the account balance is exactly Decimal('5100.00')

**`F147` — Disbursement to a closed account raises and moves no money** _(AC5, functional)_

1. Take a loan to APPROVED and close the applicant's target account
1. Call disburse inside pytest.raises for a domain error
1. Assert the error is raised and the loan status is still APPROVED
1. Assert the account balance is unchanged

**`F148` — Disbursement rejects an account that does not belong to the applicant** _(AC5, security)_

1. Take customer A's loan to APPROVED
1. Call disburse targeting an account owned by customer B inside pytest.raises
1. Assert the error is raised
1. Assert B's balance is unchanged and the loan status is still APPROVED
