E6-S2 · AC-01 onboarding

Open your account with Horizon.

Three steps: create your credentials, complete the identity check, then sign in. You cannot sign in until the identity check has verified — and we say so plainly rather than pretending your password is wrong.

2 Verify your identity

Identity check

The verdict returns immediately. Your document number is redacted from every log line.

Redacted from logs and never repeated back in an error message.

POST /api/v1/auth/kyc200 VERIFIED | REJECTED409 KYC_ALREADY_SUBMITTED

Deterministic by design

The stub verifier is deterministic, not random (A-02): a well-formed document number verifies, and the reserved synthetic value REJECT0000000 always rejects. A random stub would make the failure path untestable.

A rejected check leaves the customer in PENDING_KYC and offers no route to sign-in.

Sign in

A 30-minute bearer token is issued and attached to every subsequent request.

403 is not 401

These two failures look similar to a customer and are entirely different to the system, so they read differently on screen:

Sign-in failure distinctions
HTTPerror.codeWhat the customer reads
401UNAUTHORIZED “Those sign-in details are not correct.” — deliberately says nothing about whether the email exists.
403PENDING_KYC “Your identity check has not completed yet.” — the credentials were correct; the account is simply not active.
