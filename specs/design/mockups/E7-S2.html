E7-S2 · AC-09 & AC-10 · administrator

Audit ledger

Every financially significant event, in the order it happened, with the actor who caused it and the correlation id that ties it to the log stream. Nothing on this page can change a record.

Append-only. Entries can be read and filtered. They cannot be edited or deleted — not by an administrator, not by this interface, not by the API. Immutability is enforced at the repository, at a database trigger, and at a write-time hook. There is deliberately no edit or delete control on this screen.
