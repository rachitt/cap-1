E6-S1 · Foundation

A design system for money you can read at a glance.

Horizon Bank is a retail bank, not a novelty. The system rests on three commitments: money is the loudest element on any screen it appears on, state colour is reserved strictly for state, and no failure is ambiguous. Every screen in this pack reproduces the token block above verbatim.

Ledger, not dashboard

Rules and hairlines carry structure instead of shadows and soft cards. Radii stop at 4px. The page reads like a statement, because that is what it is.

Tabular figures, always

Every money value renders in a monospaced, tabular-numeral face so digits align down a column and a misplaced decimal is visible instantly.

Colour with a job

Green, amber and red mean success, caution and danger. They are never decorative. A balance never carries a gradient.

04 · Money

The money component

Contract: money is a decimal string

Every money field arrives as a quoted string such as "1250.00" or "0.00" and is rendered by formatMoney(), which inserts group separators and pads to exactly two decimal places by string manipulation alone. The browser performs no arithmetic on money — no parseFloat, no Number() (NFR-01, DD-05, DD-16). The currency glyph is decorative and hidden from assistive technology; the currency is announced in visually hidden text.

05.2 · Components

Feedback: alerts and the live region

Outcome messages are never a bare colour change. Each carries a heading naming the outcome, a sentence of consequence, and where relevant the machine error code from the uniform error envelope.

Transfer completed

₹250.00 sent to HZN0000000202. Reference TRF-8842-0193. Your available balance is now ₹115,221.11.

Transfer declined — insufficient funds

Your available balance is lower than the requested amount. No money has left your account and no transaction was recorded against your balance. error.code = INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS · correlation_id = 01J8ZQK7M2C4X9N0RT5VB3DHYE

Verification still pending

Your identity check has not completed, so sign-in is not yet available. error.code = PENDING_KYC · HTTP 403, not 401.

This ledger is append-only

Audit entries cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including administrators. There is no edit or delete control on that screen by design (NFR-02).

Live region

Every outcome that moves money is announced. The region exists in the DOM before the action, so assistive technology observes the mutation rather than missing it.

Idle — nothing announced yet.

aria-live="polite" on successrole="alert" on failure
